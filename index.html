01 / Engineering approach

Understand enough to act deliberately.

Complex systems rarely need more activity first. They need a clearer model of what is actually happening.

I investigate behavior, make assumptions explicit, weigh consequences, and match engineering effort to the context. The goal is not theoretical perfection. It is a system that remains understandable and capable of change.
